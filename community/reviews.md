# User Reviews & Experience Guidelines

We welcome candid feedback, performance benchmarks, and user reviews from finance professionals, auditors, and CPAs.

## How to Share Your Review
1. Go to the [GitHub Discussions: Reviews](https://github.com/Aktnyl/Hijin-AI/discussions/categories/user-reviews-experiences).
2. Click **New Discussion**.
3. Use the **User Review Template**:
   * **Your Role / Industry:** (e.g., CPA, Corporate Controller, Financial Analyst)
   * **Hardware Spec:** (RAM, CPU, GPU)
   * **Use Case Tested:** (e.g., Bank reconciliation, variance analysis, invoice cross-check)
   * **Accuracy & Speed Rating:** (1 to 5 stars)

   * **What Needs Improvement:**

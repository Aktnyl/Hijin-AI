Zero-Data Architecture

Privacy Guarantee & Data Policy

Last Updated: October 1, 2026 · Effective Immediately

Our Core Architectural Commitment:

Hijin is built from the ground up as a local-first desktop workbench. Your financial invoices, bank statements, general ledgers, prompts, and exported reports are processed exclusively on your physical workstation hardware. They are never transmitted to our servers, third-party LLM providers, or any cloud entity.

1. Complete Absence of Document Uploads

When you drag-and-drop accounting documents into Hijin, they are parsed by the local Rust daemon (hijin_daemon.exe) and indexed in an embedded local SQLite database located in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Hijin\data\. At no point in the ingestion, OCR, parsing, reconciliation, or export lifecycle are document bytes or extracted text sent over any network socket.

2. Zero Third-Party Analytics or Tracking

Both our public distribution website and our desktop application contain zero third-party tracking scripts, zero surveillance pixels, zero Google Analytics, zero Hotjar, and zero telemetry daemons. We do not track keystrokes, session durations, or document filenames.

3. Cryptographic License Verification

To prevent unauthorized duplication of commercial licenses, the desktop app checks in once every 7 days with our licensing endpoint. This request transmits strictly:

  • Your license key token (e.g. HJN-TRL-...).
  • An anonymized, salted SHA-256 one-way cryptographic hash of your system motherboard identifier.

No username, IP geolocation, file list, or business data is ever attached to licensing requests. If your workstation is offline, the app operates uninterrupted for 7 full days via cached Ed25519 tokens.

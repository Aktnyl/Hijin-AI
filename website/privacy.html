Local-First Guarantee

Privacy Policy & Data Security

Effective Date: September 2026 • Published by Hijin Tech

1. The Local-First Architecture Pledge

Hijin Tech built Hijin with a fundamental design constraint: your financial documents belong exclusively to you. Unlike cloud-hosted AI copilots, Hijin runs its language model, document ingestion, and financial arithmetic engines locally on your workstation.

What Stays on Your Machine

  • All uploaded documents (PDFs, scans, receipts, invoices, spreadsheets, TXT files).
  • All conversation history, user prompts, and AI reasoning traces.
  • All generated artifacts (PowerPoint briefings, PDF audit briefs, JSON ledgers).
  • All workspace configurations and local databases.

2. What Network Communication Occurs

Hijin communicates over the internet strictly for necessary commercial and operational functions:

  • License Synchronization: Every 24 hours (or upon manual refresh), Hijin checks the Hijin Tech commercial licensing server to validate active subscription status and download digitally signed Ed25519 entitlement envelopes.
  • Update Checks: Hijin queries the release channel endpoint to determine if a verified, cryptographically signed software upgrade is available.
  • Billing Processing: When purchasing or upgrading a plan, payment transactions are conducted through Lemon Squeezy's secure payment interface. Payment card data never touches Hijin servers.

At no point does Hijin transmit document contents, prompt texts, financial figures, or conversation transcripts over the network.

3. Data Retention and Subscription Expiry

If your trial concludes or your subscription expires without renewal:

  • Customer Data Preservation: Your local workspace, past conversations, uploaded files, and generated artifacts are never deleted or locked.
  • Export Rights: You retain permanent access to view, read, and export all previously generated files.
  • Capability Gating: New AI analysis and reconciliation prompts require an active trial or paid license.

4. Compliance & Transparency Disclaimer

We believe in honest, evidence-based claims. Hijin does not claim formal third-party compliance certifications (such as SOC 2, HIPAA, or ISO 27001) that have not been externally audited and certified. Instead, our data confidentiality guarantee is grounded in local-first isolation: data that is never sent to the cloud cannot be intercepted, leaked, or mined by cloud providers.

For inquiries regarding enterprise deployments or custom air-gapped licensing, contact privacy@hijintech.com.

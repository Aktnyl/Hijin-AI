Zero-Data Privacy Guarantee &
Data Protection Policy

Your financial documents, journal entries, supplier invoices, and audit memos never leave your workstation. Hijin is engineered from the ground up for strict confidentiality.

1. Local-Only Document Processing Guarantee

Hijin runs an embedded Rust daemon and local SQLite database entirely inside your operating system environment. When you upload or drag-and-drop PDFs, Excel sheets, or CSV files into Hijin, they are parsed, embedded, and reconciled 100% locally on your device. No document text, numbers, or metadata are ever transmitted to Hijin Tech servers or any third-party cloud AI provider.

2. Zero Tracking Cookies & Zero Website Surveillance

This website (https://aktnyl.github.io/Hijin-AI/) does not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, tracking beacons, fingerprinting scripts, or advertising cookies. We believe financial software companies should respect privacy in all aspects of their public presence.

3. Transparent Disclosure: What Network Requests Occur?

For complete transparency, here are the only two instances where the Hijin desktop application accesses the network:

  • Cryptographic License Check (Once per 7 days): The app transmits your public license key to verify active subscription status and receives back a cryptographically signed Ed25519 token. No workstation data or document names are included.
  • Model Weight Streaming: The in-app Model Hub connects directly to the Hugging Face CDN (over HTTPS) to download open-weight GGUF neural packages upon user request.

4. Air-Gapped & Offline Operation

Subscribers can disconnect their workstation from the internet or run in secure audit field facilities. The software operates with full capability for up to 7 consecutive days offline. Enterprise and Institutional tiers support perpetual air-gapped USB deployment.

5. Data Retention & Uninstallation

All application state, local databases, and temporary caches are stored in your local Windows user directory (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Hijin). Uninstalling the application or deleting this folder permanently deletes all records from your system.

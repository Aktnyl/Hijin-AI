Common Troubleshooting Steps

  • Windows SmartScreen Warning: For independent builds, click More info followed by Run anyway.
  • Ollama Runtime Prerequisite: If using an external runtime, ensure Ollama is executing and accessible on 127.0.0.1:11434.
  • Disk Space Requirements: Ensure at least 15 GB of free disk space is available for local neural model storage.
  • Offline Expiry: If the 7-day grace period concludes, connect to the internet briefly to refresh your license signature.

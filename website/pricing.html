Licensing & Commercial FAQ

What is the difference between the $99/month subscription and the $349 one-time license?

The $99/month subscription is designed for practitioners who want ongoing flexibility, priority support, and all models in the Hijin Model Store included (such as future tax, legal, and audit models) as they are released.

The $349 One-Time License is a perpetual, offline license for the complete Hijin workstation paired specifically with the Phase 5E Financial Specialist V2 model (.gguf weights and LoRA adapter). It includes 1 full year of system updates and software support. Extra models from the store can be acquired à la carte whenever needed.

Can I buy additional trained models if I choose the $349 one-time license?

Yes! If you are on the $349 Standalone License and need another specialized model (for example, Corporate Tax or Contract Review), you can purchase that specific model individually from the Model Store without having to switch to a monthly subscription.

What happens when the 14-day trial ends?

The software enters read-only evaluation mode. All parsed financial documents, audit reports, and local databases remain 100% accessible on your machine forever. You can activate either a commercial subscription or a standalone license anytime to restore active AI inference.

How does Blink checkout work?

Blink provides instantaneous, frictionless checkout. Once payment is completed, you receive your cryptographic Ed25519 license key by email immediately. Enter the key in Hijin's license dialog to activate your workstation in seconds.

Are my client documents or accounting records ever uploaded to the cloud?

Never. Hijin is strictly local-first and air-gap compliant. All document parsing, neural inference, and calculation execute on your workstation's hardware. Zero document bytes or client information leave your device.

How the 14-Day Free Evaluation Works

We believe you should evaluate Hijin on your own accounting documents before purchasing. We do not require credit card entry or complex sales calls.

  1. Download the Installer: Get Hijin_Setup_1.0.0.exe (6.58 MB) from the downloads page.
  2. Run Setup: Complete the standard Windows setup wizard (installs to your local user directory).
  3. Activate Evaluation: When the First-Launch Wizard appears, click "Start 14-Day Free Trial". A cryptographic trial token is signed locally for your workstation.
  4. Configure Models: Connect your local runtime or stream the official models in the Model Hub to start reconciling.

Licensing & Commercial FAQ

What happens when the 14-day trial ends?

The software enters read-only evaluation mode. All parsed financial documents, audit reports, and local databases remain 100% accessible on your machine. You can activate a commercial subscription anytime to restore active inference.

Are future model updates included in the $99/month price?

Yes. Eligible active subscribers receive supported Hijin model updates included with their subscription through the in-app Model Hub without additional fees.

How does the 7-day offline grace period work?

Hijin performs an online cryptographic check once every 7 days. If your workstation is offline for travel, air-gapped site visits, or secure field audits, it operates with full functionality for up to 7 consecutive days without an internet connection.

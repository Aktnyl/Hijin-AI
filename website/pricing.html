Answers to Common Questions

Licensing & Commercial FAQ

Everything you need to know about our billing, model delivery, and offline guarantees.

What happens when my 14-day trial ends?

When your trial period concludes, the desktop application enters read-only evaluation mode. Your locally stored financial ledgers, parsed documents, and exported reports remain completely intact on your machine. You can upgrade to a commercial subscription anytime to reactivate inference.

Do I have to pay extra when newer, larger AI models are released?

No! All active subscribers receive future model architecture upgrades (including upcoming 8B parameter models) directly in the in-app Model Hub at zero extra charge. You never need to purchase additional "add-on" model packs.

How does the 7-day offline grace period work?

Hijin validates your cryptographic Ed25519 license online once every 7 days. If your workstation is taken on an airplane, audit field site, or air-gapped secure facility, it functions with 100% operational capability for up to 7 full days without checking in.

Can I use my license across two workstations?

A single-user license allows activation on up to two machines (such as your office workstation and your travel laptop), provided both instances belong to the designated named licensee.

What payment methods are supported?

We process subscription billing via Lemon Squeezy and Stripe, supporting all major credit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and SEPA. For institutional multi-seat purchases ($2,500+), we support ACH wire transfers and custom invoicing.

Is my financial data truly safe?

Yes. The desktop app runs an embedded Rust daemon and SQLite database entirely on your local machine. There are zero cloud AI calls, zero analytics trackers, and zero telemetry transmissions of your accounting records.

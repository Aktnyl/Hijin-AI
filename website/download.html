Smart Delivery Architecture

Why is the Setup File Only 6.58 MB?

We engineered a modern two-tier distribution system to prevent massive multi-gigabyte browser download failures.

Tier 1: Desktop Shell & Daemon (6.58 MB)

The initial Windows installer downloads in under 3 seconds. It deploys the native Tauri 2 application, the Rust background daemon (hijin_daemon.exe), cryptographic license manager, and local SQLite document database.

Tier 2: In-App AI Model Hub (~12.3 GB)

When you launch Hijin, the built-in Model Hub streams the fine-tuned neural weights with pause, resume, background downloading, and SHA-256 chunk validation. If you already have local GGUF models or Ollama, Hijin detects and links them instantly.

Fast Setup

Step-by-Step Windows Installation

From download to your first automated reconciliation in four easy steps.

1

Download the Validated Installer

Click the download button above to get Hijin_Setup_1.0.0.exe (6.58 MB) directly from our official GitHub release CDN.

2

Run the Setup Wizard (Windows SmartScreen)

Double-click the setup file. If Windows SmartScreen displays "Windows protected your PC", click More info and select Run anyway (standard for new software builds). The installer deploys to %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Hijin without requiring Administrator privileges.

3

Activate Evaluation or Commercial License

Launch Hijin from your Start menu or Desktop shortcut. The First-Launch Wizard prompts you to enter your License Key or click "Start 14-Day Free Evaluation". An Ed25519 cryptographic token is saved locally to your system.

4

Stream Local Models & Begin Reconciling

The in-app Model Hub downloads the Dual-Core Neural Pack (~12.3 GB) or connects to your existing local Ollama instance. Drag-and-drop your bank statement CSVs and supplier invoice PDFs to begin reconciling!

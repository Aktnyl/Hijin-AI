# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to community@hijintech.com. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
